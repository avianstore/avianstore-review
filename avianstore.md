## The Standout Feature: Custom Vintage Design Restoration

Here is something you rarely see at budget-friendly apparel stores.

If you have an old photo of a faded concert t-shirt from the 90s, a blurry screenshot of a rare vintage graphic, or a worn-out favorite tee you can't find anywhere online, AvianStore offers a **[Custom Vintage Restoration Service](https://avianstore.com/contact-us/)**.

How it works in practice:
1. You email a photo, scan, or reference image to `support@avianstore.com` along with the garment style you want.
2. Their in-house graphic designers manually redraw, clean up, and vectorize the artwork to high-resolution print quality.
3. They send you a digital mockup for approval before anything goes to print.

For collectors of niche retro graphics or anyone trying to recreate a sentimental shirt, this alone makes bookmarking the site worthwhile.

## Print Quality & Production Reality

Because they use **Direct-to-Garment (DTG) printing** with eco-friendly, water-based inks, the print actually binds into the fabric fibers rather than sitting on top like a thick, rubbery plastic decal.

- **Pros of DTG:** Soft hand-feel, breathable chest area, vibrant color gradients, and strong resistance to cracking.
- **Garment Blanks:** They tailor the fabric blend to the garment type—breathable ring-spun cotton and soft blends for tees and tanks, and thicker, structured cotton-poly fleece for hoodies so they hold their shape after washing.
